# Instructions For The Game

## How to play
After running the game, you will see a game screen with 4 locations. Search for clues in each of these locations and then interrogate the suspects.
When you believe you’ve identified the murderer, use the accusation button to make your final choice.            

## Controls
- Click locations to enter.
- Click to search for clues
- Use the GUI accuse button to accuse the murderer.
  You only require a mouse to play 

## Game Rules
- You must identify the murderer using logic and not by blind guesses.
- Clues are very important in finding the correct murderer; therefore, don't ignore them.
- Being nervous to answer the question does not mean that the suspect is a murderer. They could just be overwhelmed by the questions.
- Don't overquestion the suspects. Try to ask meaningful questions.
- Your final accusation is permanent and ends the game.
